# Ahead UI direction

## Product feeling

Ahead is a personal visual try-on and fitting handoff for people navigating treatment-related hair loss. The interface should feel calm, considered and editorial: supportive without becoming clinical, and polished without looking like a generic beauty store.

## Visual system

- **Typography:** DM Sans for controls and reading; Instrument Serif for page titles and the wordmark.
- **Palette:** porcelain background, deep pine surfaces, ink text and a restrained hair-colour accent measured from the user's photo.
- **Composition:** clear product promise, typographic lookbook panel instead of a synthetic hero portrait, deliberate negative space and short action paths.
- **Motion:** soft, purposeful transitions with reduced-motion support.
- **Disclosure:** sample people and products are AI-generated; state this once in the shared footer rather than overlaying each image.

## Main flow

1. **Landing:** a memorable promise, two clear ways to enter, and a short three-step explanation.
2. **Consent:** plain-language privacy details before the camera or personal-photo flow.
3. **Capture:** upload first, camera as an alternative, sample portraits as a no-selfie route.
4. **Stage:** keep the selected photo visible beside the context question.
5. **Results:** keep the user's photo present while they switch between wigs and wraps, compare options and prepare a stylist brief.

## Quality bar

- The first screen explains the value and offers a clear action.
- Personal photos stay local until a user-requested live preview.
- Images and actions remain legible and usable on narrow screens.
- Results state what the API did and what its previews cannot promise.
- Interface copy stays cosmetic and avoids medical claims.
