# Ahead demo — target 2:20 (hackathon limit: 1–3 minutes)

## One-sentence story

Ahead uses YouCam Hair Transfer to help people navigating treatment-related hair loss see wigs and wraps on their own photo, compare favourites, and take a fitting brief to a specialist.

## Record this flow

Use a consented portrait for live previews. Generate the renders before recording so the demo stays within time. Keep the same person visible in the original and every result. Do not show the preview code, API key, email, private images, or balance screen.

| Time | On screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Landing page headline and own-photo / sample options. | “Choosing a wig or wrap can be difficult when the product photo shows someone else. Ahead lets people explore styles on their own photo before a fitting.” |
| 0:15–0:35 | Upload a consented photo, briefly show privacy details, choose the person's stated hair-loss stage. | “I choose a photo and tell Ahead where I am in this experience. Ahead does not infer that from my face. My photo is sent to YouCam only when I request a live preview.” |
| 0:35–1:05 | Show two distinct live YouCam Hair Transfer results on the same face; show image integrity line. | “YouCam Hair Transfer puts each wig or wrap direction on my photo. Ahead checks the face and skin colour against the original, so I can focus on the style.” |
| 1:05–1:30 | Show the four-column desktop catalogue at a readable viewport, include a wrap, and add two looks to compare. | “I can compare the looks I like, then save them for a fitting. These are illustrative style directions, not live retailer products.” |
| 1:30–1:58 | Open the fitting brief; show style directions and specialist checklist; use Share or Print. | “The brief carries the chosen directions, not my photo. It gives me specific questions about the closest available product, cap construction, price, care and return terms.” |
| 1:58–2:20 | Close on API name and the future retailer connection. | “Ahead makes a personal preview part of a useful shopping journey. The next step is connecting a specialist's licensed catalogue so a style direction can point to real products and availability.” |

## Backup judge path

The landing page's **Explore sample previews** link opens recorded YouCam outputs on a sample portrait. It requires no upload, code, or API units. Say that the sample view replays saved results; do not describe it as a new live inference. The organizer has confirmed that a clearly identified replay of a real API response can be used for judge access.

## Evidence card (optional)

If there is room, show: “In one 2025 survey, 157 of 202 respondents receiving systemic therapy reported hair loss; 109 of those 157 bought wigs.” Keep the respondent count and the one-study caveat visible. Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39891884/.
